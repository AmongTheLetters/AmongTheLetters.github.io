Who we are

Among The Letters is a literary newsletter for writers and readers. It gathers useful opportunities for writers and worthwhile things to read. Morning Opportunities appears on Mondays, Afternoon Discoveries arrives every day, and paid subscribers receive a Sunday evening Weekly Discoveries Roundup.

Editions

  • Morning Opportunities (Mondays): writing and editorial work, contests, grants, fellowships, residencies, and calls for submissions, checked against primary or authoritative sources.
  • Afternoon Discoveries (daily): books, authors, essays, stories, poems, magazines, and literary projects worth finding, with a useful free section and an expanded paid section.
  • Weekly Discoveries Roundup (Sunday evening, paid subscribers): a compact catch-up of the week's Featured Discoveries, day-by-day links, and timely Monday opportunities when useful.

Afternoon Discoveries genre week

  1. Monday: Literary Fiction & Poetry
  2. Tuesday: Science Fiction & Fantasy
  3. Wednesday: Mystery, Thriller, Crime, Horror, & Dark Fiction
  4. Thursday: Romance, Women's Fiction, & Historical Fiction
  5. Friday: Creative Nonfiction, Memoir, Essays, & Narrative Journalism
  6. Saturday: Speculative, Hybrid, Experimental, Short Fiction, & Flash
  7. Sunday: Emerging Writers & Worth Revisiting
