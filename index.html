Who we are

Among The Letters is a free reading newsletter for anyone who loves to read, in every genre. Every afternoon, Afternoon Discoveries brings stories, poems, and essays you can read online, picked by hand with every link checked, and each day of the week has its own genre. Paid subscribers also receive a Sunday evening Weekly Discoveries Roundup. For readers who also write, Monday Morning Opportunities and free writer resources are useful extras.

Editions

  • Afternoon Discoveries (daily): stories, poems, essays, books, and authors worth finding, free to read online, with a different genre each day, a useful free section, and an expanded paid section.
  • Weekly Discoveries Roundup (Sunday evening, paid subscribers): a compact catch-up of the week's Featured Discoveries, with day-by-day links.
  • Morning Opportunities (Mondays, for readers who write): writing and editorial work, contests, grants, fellowships, residencies, and calls for submissions, checked against primary or authoritative sources.

Afternoon Discoveries genre week

  1. Monday: Literary Fiction & Poetry
  2. Tuesday: Science Fiction & Fantasy
  3. Wednesday: Mystery, Thriller, Crime, Horror, & Dark Fiction
  4. Thursday: Romance, Women's Fiction, & Historical Fiction
  5. Friday: Nonfiction, Memoir, Essays, & Narrative Journalism
  6. Saturday: Speculative, Hybrid, Experimental, Short, & Flash Fiction
  7. Sunday: Emerging Writers, Debuts, & Reading Worth Revisiting
