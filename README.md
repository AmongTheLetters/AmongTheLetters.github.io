# Among The Letters

**A literary discovery newsletter for writers, readers, and people who like finding worthwhile things among the noise.**

Among The Letters is an independent literary newsletter dedicated to finding and sharing writing opportunities, books, authors, publications, stories, essays, poems, and other work worth discovering.

This repository contains the official website for **Among The Letters**.

## Publication Rhythm

Among The Letters now has three distinct recurring editions.

### Morning Opportunities

Published on **Mondays only**, Morning Opportunities gathers legitimate opportunities for writers, including:

- Paid writing and editorial work
- Literary contests and prizes
- Grants and fellowships
- Residencies
- Calls for submissions
- Other worthwhile opportunities for writers

Listings are selected with an emphasis on useful details, clear requirements, primary or authoritative sources, and information writers can actually use.

Regular Morning Opportunities posts contain a useful free section followed by an expanded paid section.

### Afternoon Discoveries

Published **every day, Sunday through Saturday**, Afternoon Discoveries explores the wider literary world.

That can include:

- Books and new releases
- Authors and emerging writers
- Essays, stories, and poetry
- Literary publications
- Interesting projects
- Interviews, criticism, craft, and literary journalism
- Older or overlooked work worth returning to as Special Recommendations

Afternoon Discoveries follows a seven-day genre rhythm while leaving room for an exceptional cross-genre find. The existing Sunday theme is **Emerging Writers + Worth Revisiting**.

Regular Afternoon Discoveries posts contain a useful free section followed by an expanded paid section.

### Weekly Discoveries Roundup

Published on **Sunday evenings for paid subscribers**, the Weekly Discoveries Roundup arrives in addition to the regular Sunday Afternoon Discoveries edition and provides a compact catch-up and index of the week.

It gathers:

- Every Featured Discovery from the week
- Concise day-by-day summaries with read-more links
- A small set of Monday opportunities that are still timely, when useful

The Sunday roundup is a complete paid-subscriber post rather than a free section followed by an internal paywall.

## Free and Full Editions

Among The Letters can be read for free. The free portions of regular Morning and Afternoon editions are designed to be genuinely useful on their own.

The standard paid subscription is **$5 per month** and expands the regular editions with more opportunities, discoveries, Special Recommendations, and context. Paid subscribers also receive the complete Sunday evening Weekly Discoveries Roundup.

Standard newsletter subscriptions, publishing, and billing are handled through Substack. Among The Letters also offers a separate **$100 founding lifetime membership** during this early stage. That offer includes lifetime access to the paid newsletter plus select future Among The Letters products released outside Substack. Specific future extras are intentionally not promised before those products exist; inquiries are handled directly at **amongtheletters@gmail.com**.

## Newsletter Archive

The website includes a dedicated archive at:

**https://amongtheletters.github.io/archive/**

The archive displays published editions that have been intentionally approved for public PDF access. Each archive card opens the corresponding PDF in a new browser tab.

### Adding another public archive issue

1. Place the PDF in `public/newsletters/` using a simple web-safe filename.
2. Add a matching entry to `src/lib/archive-content.ts`.
3. Rebuild the site and verify the PDF link before deployment.

**Important:** files placed in `public/newsletters/` are publicly accessible. Do not place a paid-subscriber-only Sunday roundup, or a full regular edition containing subscriber-only material, in that folder unless making that PDF public is intentional.

## About This Website

This website serves as the permanent public home and introduction to Among The Letters. It explains the publication rhythm, subscription structure, editorial approach, genre calendar, and provides access to the public PDF archive.

The newsletter itself is published through Substack. The website is intentionally lightweight and does not act as the publication backend, subscriber database, or payment system.
